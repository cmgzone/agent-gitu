About Us

The Daily Grind started as a tiny cart with one espresso machine and a big dream: bring honest, expertly roasted coffee to the neighborhood. Today we source beans directly from family farms in Colombia and Ethiopia, roast them in small batches right here in the shop, and brew every cup to order.

Whether you're grabbing a quick espresso on the way to work or settling in with a latte and a book, our baristas are here to make your day a little warmer.
