Our Story

The Daily Grind was born from a simple belief: great coffee brings people together. What started as a small cart at the local farmers market in 2015 has grown into a beloved neighborhood gathering place.

We source our beans directly from small farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, roasting them in-house every week to ensure peak freshness. Every cup is crafted by our trained baristas who treat coffee-making as an art form.

Whether you're grabbing a quick espresso on your way to work or settling in with a latte and a good book, The Daily Grind is your home away from home.
