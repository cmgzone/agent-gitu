Programs & Academics

From early learners to graduating seniors, our programs build strong foundations and encourage students to explore what excites them.

Elementary School

Playful, hands-on learning in reading, math, science, and social studies, with an emphasis on curiosity, kindness, and confidence.

Middle School

Students deepen core academic skills while exploring robotics, music, visual arts, world languages, and leadership opportunities.

High School

College-preparatory courses, advanced labs, career pathways, athletics, and counseling support for life after graduation.

About Brightwood Academy

Founded on the belief that every child deserves to be known, challenged, and encouraged, Brightwood Academy blends rigorous academics with a warm community culture.

Our teachers design lessons that connect classroom learning to the real world. Students collaborate on projects, present their ideas, and learn how to solve problems with creativity and integrity.

Our Mission

To develop thoughtful, capable learners who are ready to serve their communities and pursue their goals with courage.
