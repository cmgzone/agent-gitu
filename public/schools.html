Our demo schools

Three campuses, one shared mission: joyful, rigorous learning. Every school below is a fully equipped demo campus open for visits and enrollment previews.

A college-preparatory campus for ages 14–18 offering advanced STEM tracks, arts studios, athletics, and leadership programs.
