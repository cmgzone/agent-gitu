Cowork

A team, not
a single thread.

Give a goal to a few named teammates. Each one opens threads, reports what it found, and is held to the same evidence gate as everything else you run.

The team

Four roles, one goal

Each teammate has a distinct remit, so nobody duplicates work and nobody “handles everything.”

G

Gitu

Orchestrator. Breaks the goal into steps, delegates them, and refuses to close the task while a criterion is unproven.

R

Rhea

Researcher. Reads documentation and sources, then posts findings with citations instead of guessing.

K

Kiro

Builder. Makes the focused edits, keeps the diff small, and runs the project's own checks.

V

Vera

Verifier. Attacks the result with edge cases and probes until the evidence actually holds.

Threads, not a wall of logs

Every teammate reports into its own thread. You can follow one person, mute another, and read the history later without scrolling back through a transcript.

  • G Split the migration into four stepsGitu · plan · ledger updated 2m
  • R Found the deprecation notice in the changelogRhea · research · 3 sources cited 7m
  • K Migration applied to 6 files, tests still redKiro · code · evidence pending 11m
  • V Null session is unhandled — failing the criterionVera · review · blocker raised 4m

Rooms for the work that outlives a chat

A room is a durable place for a recurring effort: a release checklist, a flaky-test watchlist, a migration being carried across weeks. Teammates keep working in the room whether or not you are watching.

Because the task ledger persists, a room that was interrupted mid-way resumes with its plan, its criteria, and its evidence still attached.

Connections

Teammates can reach the tools you have connected, but only through registered operations. Credentials stay in secure settings and are never printed into a thread.

What the team will not do

More teammates is not more authority. Every teammate inherits the same project lock, the same bounded plan, and the same evidence gate. A teammate cannot widen its own scope, and a teammate cannot mark a criterion green by asserting it is green.

Parallel, not unbounded

Teammates work concurrently on the same locked repo. You still get one reviewable diff set, and the gate still applies to all of it.
